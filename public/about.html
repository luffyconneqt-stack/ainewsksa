This site runs on an engine built by Conneqt.

AI News KSA is a live deployment of the AI content engine that Conneqt ships for sportsbook, crypto, and digital-first operators across MENA. Every article on this site is generated, fact-checked, and published autonomously through the eight-layer pipeline below.

Editorial focus: AI for marketing and growth. Refresh cadence: continuous, with new articles published as new sources flow through the ingestion layer.

The pipeline

01 — DATA INGESTION

Data ingestion

RSS feeds from TechCrunch AI, The Verge AI, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google AI blog, ArXiv. Polled every 6 hours for new sources.

02 — BRIEF GENERATION

Brief generation

Each tracked story is assembled into a structured content brief — angle, supporting facts, target audience, target length, voice constraints.

03 — VOICE & GUARDRAILS

Voice & guardrails

Versioned system prompts encode the editorial voice (analytical, industry-focused, no marketing fluff) and topic boundaries.

04 — GENERATION

Generation

Anthropic Claude Sonnet 4.6 generates the article body from the brief. Long-form, on-tone, with internal structure.

05 — FACT-CHECK (RAG)

Fact-check (RAG)

Retrieval-augmented verification against source material catches hallucinated stats, names, dates, or company references before publish.

06 — SEO FORMATTING

SEO formatting

schema.org Article markup, internal linking to related pieces, hreflang tags, sitemap regeneration. Programmatic SEO at scale.

07 — PUBLISH

Publish

Static HTML pushed via git commit. New articles live in under 60 seconds. CDN-distributed globally.

08 — ANALYTICS FEEDBACK

Analytics feedback

Engagement signals (read time, scroll depth, return visits) feed back to the brief generator to evolve topic selection.
